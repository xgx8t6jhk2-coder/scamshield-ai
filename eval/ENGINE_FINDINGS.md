# Engine findings

These observations use the 200-sample corpus and engine 1.0.0. IDs refer to [corpus.json](corpus.json) and scores to [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md). Proposed weights are starting points for validation on an independent holdout. The corpus intentionally uses reserved phishing destinations, which limits what it measures about real hosting platforms and risky TLDs.

Scam narratives were written from scratch using scenarios described by the Central Bank and UAE Financial Intelligence Unit. The benign community messages use publicly published short URLs in original wording. [CBUAE National Fraud Awareness Platform](https://fraud.gov.ae/) · [UAE FIU fraud typology report](https://uaefiu.gov.ae/media/yvcbgeys/fraud-report-24-nov-final-sanitised-version-final.pdf) · [Abu Dhabi DCD survey](https://www.addcd.gov.ae/en/Media-Center/News/DCD-Launches-Fifth-Cycle-of-Sports-and-Physical-Activity-Survey-in-Abu-Dhabi) · [Emirates News Agency workshop announcement](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/c22sr9n-uae%E2%80%99s-55th-eid-al-etihad-invites-communities-help)

| Evidence | Proposed engine change |
| --- | --- |
| ss-093 and ss-095 score 45 for telling recipients to keep their codes private. | Split OTP matching into clauses and exclude negated sharing instructions within the matched clause. Start with `/\b(?:never\|do not\|don['’]t)\s+(?:send\|share\|give\|forward)\b/i` and `/لا\s+(?:ترسل\|تشارك\|تعط[ِى]?)\s/` applied only to the candidate match. Keep 40 for affirmative requests. |
| ss-096 scores 65 because “noon” in a delivery time is interpreted as the retailer and conflicts with the official Aramex URL. | For ambiguous brand names, require issuer context such as `/^(?:\[noon\]\|noon\s*:)/i`, an exact sender token, or an adjacent brand-specific phrase. Exclude URL spans from body-name detection and associate each brand with its nearest relevant clause before awarding the +35 mismatch signal. |
| ss-099, ss-109, ss-110, ss-121, ss-122, ss-128, ss-130 and ss-140 show that ordinary wording can cross the threshold on its own. | Set topic-only parcel, fine, job and prize hits to 12. Add 18 for an actionable request in the same clause, such as `/\b(?:pay\|send\|transfer\|deposit\|redeem)\b/i` or `/(?:ادفع\|حوّل\|حول\|أرسل\|استبدل)/`. Suppress that increment when the request points solely to a verified domain matching the claimed issuer. Keep independent credential and off-domain signals. |
| ss-103 and ss-104 are benign family chats, while ss-057–060 miss plausible requests to pay a third party. | Score standalone phone-loss and family payment phrases at 10. Use +30 for a family address or identity-change cue plus a third-party payment, beneficiary change or secrecy cue within the next two clauses. Seed candidates with `/\b(?:repair shop\|landlord\|beneficiary\|between us\|don.t call)\b/i` and `/(?:حساب\s*(?:العامل\|شخص)\|لا تخبر\|سدد.{0,12}بدل عني)/`. |
| ss-033–036 score 3 for phone-assisted UAE PASS approvals. Similar code disclosure or transfer coercion appears in ss-016, ss-027, ss-048, ss-052 and ss-088–090. | Add +38 when trusted identity or bank context combines with approval/code disclosure over a call or third-party chat. Candidate verbs include `/\b(?:read(?: me)?\|choose\|approve)\b.{0,32}\b(?:number\|prompt\|request)\b/i` and `/(?:وافق\|اختار\|اقرأ).{0,20}(?:الطلب\|الإشعار\|الرقم)/`. For purported bank or police transfers, match destination-change wording such as “holding account” or “holding IBAN” plus an imperative to transfer. |
| ss-040–042, ss-064–066 and ss-070–072 miss identity-document harvesting, upfront job fees and pay-to-release crypto. | Add +30 for document collection in an unofficial channel, such as `/\b(?:text\|send).{0,40}(?:both sides\|passport\|selfie)\b/i`. Add +28 for topic-specific payment demands using `/\b(?:uniform deposit\|release bond\|top up\|group wallet)\b/i` and `/(?:رسوم الفحص\|ضريبة التحرير\|رسوم السحب)/` when job or investment context is present. |
| ss-073, ss-075–078 miss threats in which the threat precedes the recipient or private-media context is distant from the threat. | Combine a private-media token, a publication threat and a payment demand within two adjacent clauses. Start with `/\b(?:leak\|publish\|forward\|screenshots?\|private pic)\b/i` and `/(?:بنشر\|بفضحك\|قروب الدوام\|قروب العائلة)/`. Award +36 for all three clues. For mixed or Arabizi samples, validate explicit variants such as `7awel`, `shurta` and `ma ba7ot` before adding them to narrowly scoped rules. |
| ss-079 scores 32 and ss-081–084 score 3. Charity vocabulary is too rigid and payment diversion is absent. | Keep charity vocabulary at +10 and add +23 when the same message directs payment to a personal wallet or individual account. Candidate patterns include `/\b(?:private wallet\|personal (?:collection )?account\|pay the courier)\b/i` and `/(?:حسابي الشخصي\|رقم حسابي\|محفظة (?:شخصية\|المنسق))/`. |
| ss-131, ss-133 and ss-200 are published community short links. The shortener alone is sufficient to flag them. | Set generic shortening to +14 and add +12 only with an independent risk cue such as credential solicitation or urgent payment. Keep the shortening signal visible. An offline engine cannot authenticate an arbitrary shortener destination from the sender label. |
| ss-167 and ss-168 score 24 and 34. ss-169 and ss-170 supply almost no visible risk evidence. | Retain an input-defanged indicator for investigation at weight 0 since safety teams also defang benign links. Apply +16 for actionable redirect parameters only if the nested HTTP(S) host differs from the wrapper, and consider that host's independently observed risk. Avoid blanket weight changes for generic submission or refund paths with little evidence. |
| Manual check with `https://example.org/%ZZ` throws `URIError` in URL analysis and may abort a whole message scan. | Catch URI decoding failure and fall back to the raw path and search for pattern matching. Add regression checks for malformed escapes in both entry points. |
